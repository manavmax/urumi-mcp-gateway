Logo options

One concept family: a rounded square containing an arch (a doorway) with a short horizontal line passing through it, so the mark reads as a gateway. Flat single colour, ink #1E2430 or dusty blue #4F6F94, no gradients and no sparkles.

A. Balanced arch
Recommended
24px and 40px, ink
Why this one: the arch occupies about 40 percent of the square, so the doorway, the crossbar and the rounded square still separate cleanly at favicon size. The line passes through the arch opening (not the apex), which is the only variant that keeps the gateway gesture at 16px. Corner radius 5.5 stays friendly without turning the square into a circle.
B. Tall narrow arch
24px and 40px, ink
Not chosen: the portrait square and narrow arch read as a door niche, and at 16px the crossbar crowds the arch shoulders. Kept as a fallback if the mark ever needs a taller lockup.
C. Wide low arch
24px and 40px, ink
Not chosen: the wide radius and low arch collapse into a plain rounded box below 20px, and the crossbar sits too close to the top edge. Good as an oversized watermark or illustration accent.

Usage

  • Chosen mark: option A. Drawn once as an inline SVG symbol (#i-logo) and reused in every mockup at 24px and 40px, in ink #1E2430 on light surfaces or white on the dusty blue primary.
  • Wordmark: "Urumi" in the system font stack, semibold, tight letter spacing. A small lighter "Gateway" label sits beside it on the login page only.
  • Favicon: design/mockups/favicon.svg uses a filled ink rounded square with the arch and line knocked out in white, so it reads on light and dark browser themes.
  • Minimum size 16px. Below that, drop the crossbar and keep the arch only.
  • No gradients, no sparkles, no glow, no robot or brain imagery, no purple to blue fade, no abstract swirl.
